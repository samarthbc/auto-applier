Quick start

Upload your resume and most of your details are filled in for you. Then review each section and save.

  1. Upload your resumeDetails are filled into the sections on the left. Fields filled this way are tagged "From resume".
  2. Review and saveCheck each section, fill anything missing (salary, notice period, eligibility), then click Save.
  3. Fill any applicationOn a job form, click the Auto Applier icon or press Alt+Shift+F.

AI resume parsing

Optional. With a Groq API key, resumes are read by an AI model, which handles unusual layouts much better. Your resume's text is sent to Groq when you upload it. Without a key, parsing stays on your computer. Get a free key at console.groq.com/keys.

Backup

Save your details and resume to a file, or load them in another browser. The API key isn't included.
