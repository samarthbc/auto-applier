Auto Applier test forms

Sample job application forms for trying the extension. Each one tests different things real sites do. None of them submit anywhere.

  1. Save your details in the extension first (right-click the icon → Options), or upload a resume there.
  2. Open a form below and click the Auto Applier icon, or press Alt+Shift+F.
  3. The Fill report panel in the bottom-left corner shows what was filled and what was missed. Click a missed item to jump to it. Use Clear form to try again.
